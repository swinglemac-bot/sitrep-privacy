Sitrep — Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Sitrep is a personal academic planner. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does not. The short version: your data exists to run your planner, it is not sold, it is not used for advertising, and it is not tracked across apps or websites.

What we store

Account information. Your email address and a password, managed by our authentication provider (Supabase). Passwords are stored only as cryptographic hashes by the provider; we never see or store your plain-text password.

Your planner content. The courses, grades, assignments, planning preferences, and calendar events you create in the app are stored in our database (hosted by Supabase) so the app can sync and plan for you. Access is restricted by row-level security: your content is visible only to your account. If you join a team inside the app, the team's coach can view team members' academic data as part of the app's coaching features.

Canvas calendar feed

If you connect a Canvas calendar feed, the feed URL you paste is stored only on your device. The app fetches the feed directly from Canvas; the URL and the feed contents are never sent to our servers. Imported events are used on your device to show your schedule and to plan around your commitments. You can disconnect the feed at any time in Settings, which erases the stored URL from your device.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or behavioral tracking, in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No sale of personal data, and no sharing with third parties except the hosting/authentication provider that operates the service.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or device identifiers.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app (Settings → Delete Account). Deletion is permanent: your account, profile, courses, assignments, plans, and events are removed from our database. The Canvas feed URL stored on your device is removed when you disconnect the feed or delete the app.

Children

Sitrep is intended for college students and is not directed at children under 13.
